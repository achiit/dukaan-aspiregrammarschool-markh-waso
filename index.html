About our school

A school this neighbourhood can call its own

When my cousin's children had to travel far every morning just to find a decent school, I kept thinking: why not here, on our own Main Road? That question is how Aspire Grammar School began a few years ago.

We started small, with one whiteboard, a few desks and teachers who cared. Today we teach from Playgroup to Matric, with separate boys and girls campuses, so every family is comfortable.

I still stand at the gate in the morning and greet the children by name. That is the school I wanted to build.

  • Separate boys and girls campusFamilies can enroll sons and daughters with full peace of mind.
  • Playgroup to Matric in one placeNo changing schools at every stage.
  • LED-supported classroomsLessons shown on screen, then practised on the whiteboard.
  • Qualified teaching staffTeachers chosen for subject knowledge and patience.
  • Education and protectionChildren's safety is treated as seriously as their results.
